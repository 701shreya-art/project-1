How it works

A gradient boosting model was trained on about 48,000 hourly records of the public Metro Interstate (I-94) traffic dataset, using hour, weekday, month and weather. Tested on the latest 20% of the timeline, the day-ahead version had an average error of 251 vehicles per hour against 278 for a weekday-and-hour baseline (R2 0.947 vs 0.937).

Prototype limits: one US highway, hourly resolution, typical monthly temperature assumed. Light / Moderate / Heavy compares the prediction with the dataset's 95th-percentile traffic (under 40% / 40-75% / over 75%). A pilot would retrain on local data.
